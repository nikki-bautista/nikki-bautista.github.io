Hired. Commissioned. Documented.

GLXW brought me on as a weekly designer, paid per piece. Took ad commissions on the side, built a commission queue so clients could see their status, and documented the whole process on YouTube — thousands of views, comments still arriving. All while running school and two part-time jobs.

For a long time I thought I was just making things on the internet. Turns out I was learning how I think — and I've been thinking that way ever since.
