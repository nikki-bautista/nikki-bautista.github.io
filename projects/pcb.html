Overview

A custom microcontroller board built around a 28-pin ATmega328P with a 16 MHz crystal, driving two DC motors through discrete MOSFET stages (Q1, Q2) with flyback protection. Five 3-pin sensor headers along the top edge, an ISP header for programming, a reset switch, and test points on the key nets. [Confirm: what it drives, and the context — course, competition, personal.]

Design decisions
  1. 01Discrete low-side MOSFET drive — gate resistor + pull-down per channel, flyback diode across each motor. [why over an H-bridge IC]
  2. 02Bulk capacitor at the motor supply — C5 close to the motor outputs to absorb switching transients away from the MCU.
  3. 03AVCC routing and decoupling — separate AVCC pour, decoupling at the MCU pins, test points on every net you'd need a probe on.
