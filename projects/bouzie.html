Overview

How we run it

Less "run the numbers," more "make sure the right people are talking and things actually happen." Operations is the connective tissue of an early-stage team: someone has to hold the whole picture and make the next step obvious to everyone else.

The team runs on a dynamic equity model: each founder starts with a base stake and earns more by hitting individual milestones over the first year, rather than splitting equity evenly on day one.
